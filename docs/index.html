⭐ Our 6 Unique Features
FEATURE 1 Active (Q: 0.35x)
Dynamic Process-Noise Adaptation
Instead of keeping uncertainty fixed, AI changes it according to the driving situation. Smooth road → lower uncertainty; bumpy road or sharp turn → higher uncertainty.
Mechanism: Predicts continuous scaling factor $\alpha_Q \in [0.1, 10.0]$ from IMU spectral roughness, preventing Kalman filter blowup from spurious shocks.
FEATURE 2 Standby (Cruising)
Stop-Based Drift Correction
When the vehicle stops, the system gets an opportunity to estimate the accumulated drift by comparing the estimated journey with the road map. It can correct the previous A → B trajectory before the vehicle continues toward C.
Mechanism: Generalized Likelihood Ratio standstill test (ZUPT) combined with Rauch-Tung-Striebel (RTS) retroactive smoothing.
FEATURE 3 Dual Tripwire: Nominal
AI Fallback Mechanism
We don't blindly trust AI. If the AI becomes uncertain because of abnormal sensor data, sudden phone movement or an unfamiliar situation, the system temporarily falls back to the traditional IEKF, keeping navigation alive.
Mechanism: Dual tripwire evaluates epistemic uncertainty ($\sigma_{ai} > 0.45$) and sudden phone handling, keeping navigation safely alive.
FEATURE 4 Seamless Damping
Seamless GNSS Switching
GNSS and dead reckoning work together when GNSS is available. When GNSS disappears, the system automatically relies more on IMU, AI and map-based positioning. When GNSS returns, it smoothly corrects the accumulated error instead of suddenly jumping to the GPS location.
Mechanism: Exponential innovation damping ($\beta(t) = 1 - e^{-3t/\tau}$) eliminates teleportation jumps over 2.5 seconds.
FEATURE 5 95% Ellipse Active
Confidence-Aware Navigation
The system can provide not only a position but also an indication of how confident it is in that position and how much error may exist.
Mechanism: Real-time 2D covariance eigen-decomposition extracting 95% horizontal confidence error ellipses ($\pm \text{meters}$).
FEATURE 6 Visual Odometry Ready
Optional Camera Assistance
If needed, the smartphone camera can provide an additional movement estimate using visual information alongside IMU data.
Mechanism: Monocular optical flow speed updates ($z_{cam} = v_{flow}$) bounding longitudinal velocity drift rate during prolonged blackouts.
